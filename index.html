Our Story

Carved from
Tradition,
Built for
the World.

Incorporated in 2007, NIVORA Granites has grown from a regional manufacturer to an internationally recognized exporter of premium Indian granite. We specialize in finished slabs and tiles, supplying leading construction firms across Poland, Turkey, Qatar, and Latin America.

Every slab passes through 100% quality control checks — from quarry selection to container loading — ensuring world-class consistency that our clients trust, order after order.
